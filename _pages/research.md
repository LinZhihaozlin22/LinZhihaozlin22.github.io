## Current research

Independent research on adaptive LLM generation, representation learning, and inference-time adaptation.

---

## Research interests

### Adaptive generation and inference-time methods

How the decisions a language model makes at inference time — rather than only what it learned during training — can be adapted to the demands of a given input, and what its internal representations support in that regard.

### LLM and AI systems

Where my engineering background and research interests meet. Methods are only useful if they hold up under real workloads and honest measurement.

- Retrieval-grounded systems
- Tool-integrated LLM workflows and structured outputs
- Evaluation and reliability, including the limits of LLM-as-judge protocols
- Scalable infrastructure for training and serving

### Agents and embodied intelligence

**Forward-looking interests, not completed work.** I have not published in these areas; I am listing them because they are where I would like to take the representation-level questions above.

- Agentic systems that plan and act over long horizons
- Multimodal grounding
- Embodied settings, where context is something a system perceives and acts within rather than only reads
