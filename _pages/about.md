I am a Research Assistant at the University of Virginia, working on large language models, adaptive generation, representation learning, and AI systems. My research studies how language models can adapt their generation to the input at inference time, and what their internal representations make possible in that regard.

This follows over three years as a Software Development Engineer at Amazon Web Services, where I worked on Amazon Connect: large-scale data and metrics platforms, distributed backend systems, and retrieval-grounded, tool-integrated LLM workflows in production. That experience shapes how I approach research. I look for methods that hold up at scale, and for evaluation that is honest about what it does and does not show.

I received an M.S. in Computer Science from Santa Clara University and a B.S. in Computer Science, *cum laude*, from the University at Buffalo.

**Research interests:** Large Language Models, AI Systems, AI Agents, Representation Learning, and Multimodal / Embodied AI.
