I am an independent researcher working on large language models, adaptive generation, representation learning, and AI systems.

My current work is independent [research](/research/) on adaptive LLM generation, representation learning, and inference-time adaptation.

This follows over three years as a Software Development Engineer at Amazon Web Services, where I worked on large-scale data and metrics platforms for Amazon Connect, along with retrieval-grounded and tool-integrated LLM workflows. That background shapes how I approach research: I am drawn to methods that survive contact with scale, and to evaluation that is honest about what it does and does not show.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Santa Clara University and a B.S. in Computer Science from the University at Buffalo.

**Research interests:** Large Language Models · AI Systems · AI Agents · Representation Learning · Multimodal and Embodied AI
